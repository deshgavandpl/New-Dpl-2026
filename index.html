Tournament Rules

Match Format

Each match will consist of 6 overs per side. The powerplay will be applied for the first 2 overs where only 2 fielders are allowed outside the 30-yard circle.

Player Eligibility

All players must be officially registered on this portal before the deadline and must be a domicile of Deshgavhan. Non-registered players will not be allowed to participate in any official DPL match.

Umpire Decision

The on-field umpire's decision is final and binding. Any aggressive dissent towards umpires will result in immediate penalty runs and potential player suspension.
